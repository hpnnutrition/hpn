## Business model
Integrative / holistic pharmacy. We blend conventional pharmacy care with nutrition and naturopathic
care, optimizing **medications, supplements, and lifestyle together** — a root-cause, whole-person model.

## Services
- **Pharmacy (pharmacist-led):** medication reviews, minor ailments prescribing, supplement safety
  checks (drug–supplement interactions), chronic disease support, vaccinations.
- **Nutrition (Registered Holistic Nutritionist):** customized plans for weight management, gut health,
  hormonal balance, and chronic disease support.
- **Naturopathic care:** root-cause approach — hormone balance, digestive health, stress management,
  chronic conditions, using natural therapies + evidence-informed protocols.
- **Products:** trusted mid-range and **practitioner-/professional-grade supplements**.

## Team
Pharmacist-led, with a **Registered Holistic Nutritionist** and naturopathic practitioner(s). Pharmacist
and nutrition team review medications and supplements together for safety and effectiveness.

## Target audience
Bradford-area patients wanting integrative, personalized care; people on medications who want to optimize
results with supplements + lifestyle; those seeking a natural, root-cause path under professional supervision.

## Brand voice
Warm, holistic, root-cause, patient-centered, integrative, accessible — but grounded in pharmacist
credibility and "evidence-informed" language. Educational, never hypey. Whole-person, not transactional.

## Compliance notes (Ontario pharmacy)
- Keep health claims general, educational, and evidence-informed; no guarantees or disease-cure claims.
- Avoid specific Rx drug brand promotion and specific dosing in mass emails.
- Pharmacist should give final sign-off before any send (OCP advertising standards).
