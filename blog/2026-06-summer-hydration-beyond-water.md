# Summer Hydration Beyond Water: A Whole-Person Guide to Staying Energized

When the Bradford summer heat settles in, "drink more water" becomes the advice of the season — and it's good advice. But if you've ever finished a tall glass and still felt foggy, sluggish, or headachy, you already know hydration is more nuanced than the glass in your hand.

At WellnessRX, we take a holistic, root-cause approach to health and wellness, and hydration is a perfect example of why the whole picture matters. Water is the vehicle, but it's the *balance* — minerals, food, timing, and how your body is actually using fluid — that keeps your energy steady through a hot day.

## Why water alone sometimes isn't enough

Your cells don't just need fluid; they need fluid they can *hold onto* and use. That's where electrolytes — sodium, potassium, magnesium, and others — come in. When you sweat through a Simcoe County heatwave or a long day in the garden, you lose minerals along with water. Replacing only the water can leave you feeling "watered down" rather than restored.

Signs your hydration may need more than plain water:
- Persistent afternoon fatigue, even when you're drinking plenty
- Muscle cramps or twitches after activity or heat
- Headaches that arrive late in the day
- Lightheadedness when you stand up quickly

## A holistic approach to staying hydrated

**Start with food, not just fluids.** Many of summer's best foods are hydration in disguise — cucumber, watermelon, berries, leafy greens, and citrus all carry water *plus* the minerals your body needs to use it. Building meals around fresh, water-rich whole foods is one of the simplest ways to support whole-body hydration.

**Mind your minerals.** A pinch of quality sea salt and a potassium-rich food (think banana, avocado, or cooked greens) can do more for steady energy than another plain glass of water. For some people — especially those who are very active, sweat heavily, or take certain medications — a thoughtfully chosen electrolyte support can help.

**Time it across the day.** Sipping steadily beats gulping a litre at once. Your body absorbs fluid more effectively in smaller, regular amounts — and it's gentler on energy and digestion.

**Watch the hidden dehydrators.** Coffee, alcohol, and very salty processed snacks all nudge your balance in the other direction. You don't have to give them up — just pair them with extra water and minerals.

## A note on medications and supplements

This is where bringing together traditional pharmacy with natural wellness under one roof really pays off. Certain medications — including some blood pressure medications and diuretics ("water pills") — change how your body manages fluid and minerals. If you take a daily medication and you're planning to add an electrolyte product or ramp up summer activity, it's worth a quick supplement safety check so everything works together safely.

That's the kind of question our pharmacist and nutrition team review together — medications, supplements, and lifestyle, all in one conversation.

## Your simple summer hydration checklist
- Build at least one water-rich whole food into every meal
- Add a mineral source (not just water) on hot or active days
- Sip steadily rather than chugging
- Balance coffee and alcohol with extra fluids
- Check in with us before adding electrolyte supplements if you take daily medications
