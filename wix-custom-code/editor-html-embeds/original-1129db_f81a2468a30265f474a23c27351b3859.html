What OHIP covers

Pharmacist assessments for all 28 authorized minor ailments and MedsCheck reviews are free to anyone with a valid Ontario health card. Flu shots are free for anyone who lives, works or studies in Ontario, and smoking cessation support is free for Ontario Drug Benefit recipients. No Ontario health card? The minor ailment assessment is $19.

What your benefits plan may cover

Nutrition, naturopathic and food sensitivity services are paid at the time of your visit. Naturopathic visits are reimbursed by many extended health plans, and some plans cover nutrition counselling. We give you a detailed receipt to submit. Check your plan before booking if reimbursement matters to you.

Magnesium Bisglycinate

Why we stock it

The gentlest common form on the digestive tract. It's the one we recommend when someone has tried magnesium citrate and given up on it.

Best for

Sleep quality, muscle cramps, stress load. Often the first thing we suggest for people who wake at 3am.

How to take it

200–400 mg elemental, evening, with or without food. Give it two to three weeks.

Medication cautions

Separate by 2 hours from levothyroxine and from tetracycline or quinolone antibiotics. Check with us first if you have reduced kidney function.

Pickup & shipping

In stock in Bradford today. Free local delivery on $75+, free GTA shipping on $99+, free Canada-wide shipping on $179+.

Questions we hear every day

Good to know before you visit

Can a pharmacist prescribe for minor ailments in Ontario?

Yes. Since July 1, 2026, Ontario pharmacists can assess and, where appropriate, prescribe for 28 minor ailments, including UTIs, pink eye, cold sores, acne, allergies and acid reflux. At WellnessRX it's a 30-minute visit with Phillip Kim, PharmD, free with a valid Ontario health card ($19 without one). Book an assessment.

How do I transfer my prescription to WellnessRX?

Fill in our prescription transfer form with a photo of your bottle or the name of your current pharmacy. We contact them, move everything over and can set up blister packs if you want them. There's no cost to transfer.

Is a nutrition consultation covered by OHIP?

No. Nutrition visits with Nancy Tran, RHN are paid privately: $120 for a 60-minute in-person consultation, $75 virtual, and $65 for a 30-minute follow-up. Some extended health plans cover nutrition counselling, so we give you a detailed receipt to submit. See nutrition consultations.

What's the difference between seeing the pharmacist and the nutritionist?

Phillip, our pharmacist, handles prescriptions, minor ailments, medication reviews and vaccines. Nancy, our Registered Holistic Nutritionist, builds food, lifestyle and supplement plans for goals like hormones, gut health, weight and pregnancy. They work together, so your nutrition plan is checked against your medications.

Will you check my supplements against my medications?

Yes. Every product on our Pharmacist's Picks shelf is screened against common prescriptions, and you can book a 30-minute Supplement Review ($55) where we go through everything you take.

Do you offer flu shots?

Yes. Flu shots are free for anyone who lives, works or studies in Ontario, and COVID vaccines are available based on eligibility. Book a vaccination appointment.

What is a MedsCheck and who qualifies?

A MedsCheck is a free 30-minute, one-on-one review of all your medications with a pharmacist. It's for Ontario residents taking three or more prescription medications for chronic conditions. Book a MedsCheck.

I'm not sure what I need. Where do I start?

Book the free 15-minute wellness call. It's a quick, no-obligation chat that points you to the right service, whether that's the pharmacist, the nutritionist or neither.

Still not sure which one you need?

Book a free 15-minute wellness call. We'll tell you whether it's a pharmacist visit, a nutrition consult, or something you can fix without buying anything at all.
