Processing and delivery times

  • Processing: most orders are packed and shipped within 2–3 business days. Orders aren't processed on Sundays or holidays.
  • Local Bradford delivery: usually within 1–2 business days.
  • GTA, Ontario and Canada-wide shipping: usually 2–7 business days after the order ships, depending on where you live.
  • Tracking: you'll get an email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships.

Holiday closures: we're closed on all Ontario public holidays (including Civic Holiday) and on the Saturday of holiday long weekends. Orders placed then ship on the next business day.

Prescription delivery

Prescription medications are not shipped by mail. Local patients can get same-day prescription delivery in Bradford and nearby. To set it up, call 905-775-7874 or send a refill or transfer request.

  • Delivered only within Ontario, to the patient or another adult at the address.
  • Some medications, such as fridge items and controlled substances, may need a signature or an in-store pickup.
  • Seniors and caregivers: see our prescription delivery for seniors page.

Lost, late or damaged orders

  1. Check your tracking link. Carriers can take an extra day or two in winter weather or busy seasons.
  2. Contact us within 7 days of delivery if something arrives damaged, is missing or isn't what you ordered. Send your order number and a photo to info@wellnessrxpharmacy.ca.
  3. We'll make it right with a replacement or a refund, including any shipping you paid.

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We can't be responsible for orders sent to an address that was entered incorrectly.

Shipping questions

How much do I need to spend for free shipping?

Local delivery in Bradford is free on orders of $75 or more. Shipping within the GTA and the rest of Ontario is free on orders of $99 or more. Shipping anywhere in Canada is free on orders of $129 or more. Below those amounts, the shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

Do you deliver prescriptions?

Yes. Local patients can get same-day prescription delivery in Bradford. Call 905-775-7874 to set it up. Prescriptions are delivered only within Ontario and are not shipped by mail.

Can I pick up my online order in store?

Yes. Choose in-store pickup at checkout and collect your order at 1–152 Holland St E, Bradford West Gwillimbury, during store hours. We'll let you know when it's ready.

Do you ship outside Canada?

Not at this time. Online orders ship to addresses in Canada only.

What if my order arrives damaged or something is missing?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery at info@wellnessrxpharmacy.ca or 905-775-7874 with your order number and a photo. We'll replace the item or refund it, including any shipping you paid.

Questions about an order?

Call or email us and a real person at the pharmacy will help. Mon 10–6, Tue/Thu/Fri 9–6, Wed 10–7, Sat 9–3.
